Mechanism

A measured stream,
not a flood.

ATTN001 selectively activates the presynaptic D2 autoreceptor. Chronic activation internalizes these receptors, releasing their brake on dopamine synthesis — a sustained, physiologic increase across the prefrontal cortex. Fundamentally different from amphetamines.

ATTN001 activating D2 receptors in live HEK293A cells
Next Program

A dual GLP-1R / TAAR1 agonist for substance use disorder.

GLP-1R agonists are showing 50–56% lower risk of alcohol use disorder. TAAR1 agonism blunts the reinforcing effects of stimulants. We are designing a single molecule that engages both receptors simultaneously. No existing therapy offers this combination.
